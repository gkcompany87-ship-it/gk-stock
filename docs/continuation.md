## Implemented design choices to preserve

Money/quantity decimal strings and BigInt scaled calculations; explicit stock row locking; durable actor/body-bound idempotency; no worker financial serialization or cross-user history; source-linked compensating reversals; frozen issued documents/original PDF bytes; invoice-row-locked payments; source-unique full conversions; exact year sequences; immutable audit; no offline stock mutation queue; company-scoped data and reference checks.

## Explicit v1 limitations, not hidden TODOs

No purchase-order/supplier module, reservation engine, stock transfer document, lot/serial/expiry tracking, partial fulfilment/invoicing, statutory credit-note model, bank integration, public customer portal, online payment gateway, second language, or visual template designer. One default warehouse is seeded; additional warehouses are modeled and selectable. Company is selected by deployment slug; there is no tenant onboarding UI. Document-mail delivery is at-least-once. Original PDFs retain issue-time payment information. Generic sorting/filtering is not implemented for every conceivable field on every module.

PWA private screens are not available offline. Browser tests use manual scan input; camera hardware decoding remains a target-device test. The accessibility design has not been independently audited. The current server module renders PDFs with bounded in-process concurrency; higher load may justify moving rendering to a BullMQ worker without splitting business services into microservices.
